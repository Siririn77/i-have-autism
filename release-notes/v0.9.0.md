# Release 0.9.0: ultra mode — the graph written out in full

Aggressive mode guarantees that nothing disappears from the graph. Ultra mode answers the next question:
**what is inside each node.** Not the connections — the composition.

## The five fields

`graph_expand.py` builds a companion file with one section per node:

| field | what it answers |
|---|---|
| `входит` | what the node is made of — the list of parts, not a summary |
| `чем доказано` | what carries it: documents, statistics, testimony — which kind |
| `что из этого следует` | what follows for the argument of the work |
| `чем показано` | what the reader or viewer actually sees: map, chart, document, scene |
| `что исключает` | what the node deliberately leaves out, and where that lives instead |

Plus one line per edge — `механизм` — because "causes" is a claim, and a claim without a mechanism is a
label. Plus two lines per axis: what it covers and what it does not. `answers` edges are structural and
need no mechanism.

## The commands

```
python3 skills/i-have-autism/scripts/graph_expand.py --new graph.md -o graph-expanded.md
python3 skills/i-have-autism/scripts/graph_expand.py --check graph.md graph-expanded.md
```

Exit 1 on a missing section, an empty field, a `TODO`, or a section the graph does not have. Sections are
matched by node id, so a renamed label does not orphan the text — but a mismatched label is still reported.

## Worked on the Second World War graph

19 nodes, 25 mechanisms, 5 axes — 130 fields, all filled, `ИТОГ ЧИСТО`. What the pass produced that the
graph alone did not:

- **"что исключает" is the field that does the work.** Writing it down forced every node to state where
  its left-out material lives: China before 1941 → the register's separate season; the Battle of the
  Atlantic → a separate line; exact casualty figures → the node on counting. A node that cannot say what
  it excludes is a node that has not decided its own borders.
- **"чем показано" caught two nodes with nothing to show.** A claim that cannot name a map, a chart, a
  document or a scene is a claim that will be narrated over a talking head — and the rule against talking
  heads already forbids that.
- **"входит" turns a heading into a plan.** The difference between "Холокост" as a label and its seven
  parts is the difference between a promise and a part.

## Cost

The most expensive of the three modes: the text runs several times the graph. It belongs on the nodes of
the part being written, not on a fifty-node master graph, unless the master graph is itself the deliverable.
